ARMS SIMULATION KIT - try enrollment, grade uploads and the DepEd school forms
==============================================================================

Everything in these files is fictional. Every LRN starts with 9999 (no real learner's
LRN does), contact numbers use the unassigned 0900 prefix, and the school is the demo
school (School ID DEMO-ARMS, SY 2026-2027, Region XI, Panabo City).

Sign in with the demo accounts (the demo password you already use for them).


THE FILES
---------
SF1_JHS_Grade-7_Ilang-Ilang_SY2026-2027.xls   (and the same as .xlsx)
    Junior High School Register as LIS exports it: Grade 7 Ilang-Ilang, 20 male and
    22 female learners with address, parents or guardian, contact number, and REMARKS
    codes T/I (transferred in), LE (late enrollment), B/A (Balik-Aral), CCT and LWD.

SF1_SHS_Grade-11_STEM-C_SY2026-2027.xlsx
    Senior High School Register (SF1-SHS): Grade 11 STEM-C, Academic - STEM,
    1st Semester, 19 male and 21 female learners; REMARKS T/I, LE, CCT and B/A.

ECR_MATH7_Grade-7-Ilang-Ilang_Q2_filled.xlsx
    DepEd e-class record of Mathematics 7 for Ilang-Ilang with Quarter 2 filled in.
    One learner has no quarterly assessment score yet, so the template gives no grade.

ECR_MAPEH7_Grade-7-Ilang-Ilang_Q2_filled.xlsx
    MAPEH 7, Quarter 2: Music, Arts, PE and Health each filled; the MAPEH grade is
    the rounded average of the four.

ECR_GENMATH11_Grade-11-STEM-C_Q2_filled.xlsx
    Strengthened SHS e-class record (SSHS v2026) of General Mathematics, a Core
    Subject (written work 25%, performance tasks 50%, quarterly assessment 25%),
    Quarter 2 filled. Two learners have no quarterly assessment score yet.

The e-class records were made the way ARMS hands them to a teacher (names in DepEd
order, each learner's LRN in a hidden column) and were saved by Excel, so they also
carry Excel's own grades. ARMS computes the same grade as Excel for every learner.

Quarter 2 is used because Quarter 1 of the demo school year is already locked.

Your own test files in this folder (the STEM-CURPOZ copy and the grades folder) were
left as they are.


STEP 1 - ENROLL THE SECTIONS (registrar: elena.ramirez@demo.arms.edu.ph)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Enrollment > drop SF1_JHS_Grade-7_Ilang-Ilang_SY2026-2027.xls (or .xlsx) >
   Read and review.
2. Check the review: Grade 7 Ilang-Ilang "will be created", 42 new learners, the
   REMARKS codes. Choose the adviser (for example Liza Mercado) > Confirm and enroll.
3. Do the same with SF1_SHS_Grade-11_STEM-C_SY2026-2027.xlsx (adviser for example
   Teresita Aquino). ARMS creates Grade 11 STEM-C with the STEM strand.
4. Upload the same SF1 again: every learner shows "Already here" and nothing is
   duplicated.

STEP 3 - UPLOAD THE GRADES (each teacher)
-----------------------------------------
Class records > Upload grades > choose the class and Quarter 2 > choose the file >
check the review > Save.
    maria.santos@demo.arms.edu.ph     ECR_MATH7_...      41 grades, 1 without a QA score
    joselito.ramos@demo.arms.edu.ph   ECR_MAPEH7_...     42 MAPEH grades
    roberto.mendoza@demo.arms.edu.ph  ECR_GENMATH11_...  38 grades, 2 without a QA score

A teacher can also start from a blank record of the class: Class records > the
"..." menu of the class > E-class record for this class. Senior High classes get
the SSHS template; MAPEH classes also get "MAPEH final grades" in that menu.

Try a mistake: open a record in Excel, type a score above its highest possible
score, save, and upload it. ARMS keeps that learner out and says why.


STEP 4 - THE SCHOOL FORMS
-------------------------
Registrar > School Forms. Choose a form, then a section, month, semester or level,
and Download. The forms are DepEd's own templates filled from ARMS:
    SF1  School Register             SF5  Promotion (Grades 7-10) / SF5A (Senior High)
    SF2  Daily Attendance            SF5B SHS completers (Grade 12)
    SF3  Books Issued and Returned   SF6  Summarized Promotion Report
    SF4  Monthly Learner Movement    SF7  Personnel Assignment List
    Summary of Final Grades (Grades 7-10)

Good ones to try:
- SF1 for Grade 7 Ilang-Ilang and for Grade 11 STEM-C (1st Semester).
- SF2 and SF4 for October 2026. A new section has no attendance records yet, so its
  SF2 shows the names and school days; Grade 7 Rosal shows the absence and tardy
  totals too.
- SF5, SF5A, SF6 and the Summary of Final Grades for SY 2025-2026, the school year
  with all four quarters graded (pick it at the top of the page).

Advisers find the forms of their advisory class (SF1, SF2, SF3, SF5 and the summary)
at the bottom of Class records.
