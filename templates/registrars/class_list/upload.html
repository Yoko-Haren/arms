What ARMS reads from the SF1

The section

School ID and name, school year, grade level and section. Senior High also gives the semester, the track and strand, and the course.

Each learner

LRN, name (last, first, extension, middle), sex, birth date, birth place, mother tongue, IP group and religion.

Family

Current address, father, mother, the guardian when the learner does not live with a parent, and their contact number.

REMARKS codes

T/I transferred in, T/O transferred out, DRP dropped, B/A Balik-Aral, LE late enrollment, CCT, LWD or LWE, ACL.

  2. Every learner is checked: a wrong LRN, a missing name or sex, the same LRN twice, or a learner already in another section.
  3. Details only get better: an empty cell in the SF1 never erases what ARMS already has.
