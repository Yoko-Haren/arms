Which file to upload

The section's SF1 exactly as LIS exports it. ARMS finds the columns by their headings, so nothing needs to be moved or deleted.

{% include 'registrars/class_list/_sf1_keys.html' %}

Each learner's row also gives the birth place, mother tongue, IP group, religion, address, parents or guardian with their contact number, and the REMARKS codes (T/I, T/O, DRP, B/A, LE, CCT, LWD, LWE, ACL).

  • You see the section first: whether it is new, its strand, and who will advise it.
  • Every learner is checked: a wrong LRN, a missing name or sex, the same LRN twice, or a learner already in another section.
  • Details only get better: an empty cell in the SF1 never erases what ARMS already has.
