The grade forecaster

Every forecast on the School Head pages comes from this model: an XGBoost model that learns from the grades of every school in ARMS. Retraining it is yours as the System Administrator, because one run learns from all schools at once. ARMS also retrains it by itself whenever a registrar locks a grading period, since locked grades are final.
